# 🌱 Nawwa | نواة — Plant a Tiny Seed, Grow Across Every Pillar

**Nawwa (نَوَاة)** is a bilingual (`English` & `العربية`) behavioral psychology & habit-building PWA designed to defeat procrastination, low motivation, smoking/bad habits, emotional eating, workout skipping, and strengthen faith (`القرآن الكريم والصلوات`) and family kinship (`صلة الرحم`).

## ✨ Core Features
- **🎯 Anti-Overwhelm "Just 1 Seed Now" Spotlight:** Highlights one 2-minute Bronze step so you never feel paralyzed by decision fatigue.
- **🥉🥈🥇 3-Tier Adaptive Goals (Bronze / Silver / Gold):** Earn `+10`, `+25`, or `+50` points—plus a 1-tap **Guilt-Free Low-Energy Mode** so you never break your streak on tough days.
- **🕌 6 Core Life Pillars + Custom Pillar Builder:**
  1. **Faith & Worship (`الجانب الديني والروحي`)** — Daily Quran reading (`1 page / 5 pages / 1 Juz` + 604-page Khatmah tracker), Daily Prayers & Sunnah, and Adhkar.
  2. **Focus & Anti-Procrastination (`التركيز ومكافحة التسويف`)** — 2-minute micro-starts & deep work blocks.
  3. **Fitness & Workout (`اللياقة والنشاط البدني`)** — Adaptive workouts from 5 pushups to 45-min sessions.
  4. **Healthy Weight & Nutrition (`الصحة والوزن المثالي`)** — Mindful eating & daily hydration tracker.
  5. **Quit Smoking & Bad Habits (`الإقلاع عن التدخين والعادات السلبية`)** — Craving control & money saved calculator.
  6. **Family Gathering & Kinship (`صلة الرحم والتجمع العائلي`)** — Warm calls, phone-free family time, and gatherings.
- **🎙️ 2-Way Voice & AI Life Coach:** Speaks aloud in natural Arabic & English, listens to Egyptian Arabic (`ar-EG`), Standard Arabic (`ar-SA`), and English (`en-US`), and executes app actions automatically.
- **📅 Smart Calendar & Notifications:** Real browser/system notifications, Google Calendar 1-click links, and `.ICS` calendar export.
- **🚨 SOS Rescue Mode:** 3-Minute Urge Surfing breathing timer + 5-Minute Procrastination Breaker.
- **🏆 Champion Board & Squad Peer Pressure:** Global & Family/Squad leaderboards with **Nudge (`تنبيه التزام`)** and **Cheer (`تشجيع`)** actions.
