# 🌱 Nawwa | نواة — Plant a Tiny Seed, Grow Across Every Pillar

**Nawwa (نَوَاة)** is a bilingual (`English` & `العربية`) behavioral psychology & habit-building PWA designed to defeat procrastination, low motivation, smoking/bad habits, emotional eating, workout skipping, and strengthen faith (`القرآن الكريم والصلوات`) and family kinship (`صلة الرحم`).

## ✨ Core Features
- **🎯 Anti-Overwhelm "Just 1 Seed Now" Spotlight:** Highlights one 2-minute Bronze step so you never feel paralyzed by decision fatigue.
- **🥉🥈🥇 3-Tier Adaptive Goals (Bronze / Silver / Gold):** Earn `+10`, `+25`, or `+50` points—plus a 1-tap **Guilt-Free Low-Energy Mode** so you never break your streak on tough days.
- **🕌 6 Core Life Pillars + Custom Pillar Builder:**
  1. **Faith & Worship (`الجانب الديني والروحي`)** — Daily Quran reading (`1 page / 5 pages / 1 Juz` + 604-page Khatmah tracker), Daily Prayers & Sunnah, and Adhkar.
  2. **Focus & Anti-Procrastination (`التركيز ومكافحة التسويف`)** — 2-minute micro-starts & deep work blocks.
  3. **Fitness & Workout (`اللياقة والنشاط البدني`)** — Adaptive workouts from 5 pushups to 45-min sessions.
  4. **Healthy Weight & Nutrition (`الصحة والوزن المثالي`)** — Mindful eating & daily hydration tracker.
  5. **Quit Smoking & Bad Habits (`الإقلاع عن التدخين والعادات السلبية`)** — Craving control & money saved calculator.
  6. **Family Gathering & Kinship (`صلة الرحم والتجمع العائلي`)** — Warm calls, phone-free family time, and gatherings.
- **🎙️ 2-Way Voice & AI Life Coach:** Speaks aloud in natural Arabic & English (Cloud Neural TTS + Device TTS), listens to Egyptian Arabic (`ar-EG`), Standard Arabic (`ar-SA`), and English (`en-US`), and executes app actions automatically (`[ACTION:COMPLETE_HABIT]`, `[ACTION:OPEN_QURAN]`, `[ACTION:OPEN_ADHKAR]`, `[ACTION:OPEN_FOCUS_TIMER]`, `[ACTION:ADD_EVENT]`, `[ACTION:OPEN_SOS]`).
- **📖 In-App Holy Quran Mushaf Reader & Audio Recitation:** Read any of the 604 Mushaf pages in Uthmani script, tap any Ayah or full page to listen to Mishary Rashid Alafasy recitation (`ar.alafasy`), toggle English translation, and log page completion with 1 tap.
- **📿 Interactive Smart Tasbih & Morning/Evening Adhkar Counter:** Digital Tasbih ring with haptic vibration, acoustic bead ticks, authentic Morning/Evening Adhkar (`أذكار الصباح والمساء`) and 33x Tasbih collections, and automatic `faith_adhkar` habit completion.
- **⏱️ Built-In Pomodoro & Deep Work Timer + Focus Soundscapes:** 2m (Bronze), 15m (Silver), 25m (Pomodoro), and 45m (Gold) countdown timer with zero-asset Web Audio synthesized **Calm Rain / Brown Noise** and **40Hz Gamma Focus Wave** soundscapes.
- **🌱 Living "Nawwa" Tree Evolution & 🛡️ Streak Shields:** Your seed evolves across 5 visual stages (`نواة -> برعم -> شتلة -> شجرة مورقة -> نخلة مثمرة`) as your points grow, and craftable **Streak Shields** automatically protect your daily chain if you miss a day.
- **🕌 Local Prayer Times & Smart Calendar Sync:** Auto-fetches daily Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha timings via Aladhan API (by City or GPS), highlights the next upcoming prayer, and syncs all 5 prayers into your Smart Schedule with voice & browser notifications.
- **📊 Weekly Growth & AI Insights + 📥 Full JSON Backup & Restore:** Calculates 7-day consistency, Quran Khatmah completion pace, money & life minutes saved from cigarettes avoided, and lets you export or restore your full `.json` backup anytime.
- **🚨 SOS Rescue Mode & 🧠 Brain Gym:** 3-Minute Urge Surfing breathing timer, 5-Minute Procrastination Breaker, and 3 cognitive focus games (Sequence Memory, Stroop Reflex, Pillars Match).
- **🏆 Champion Board, 📲 1-Tap WhatsApp Sharing & 🗄️ Permanent Cloud Sync:** Global & Family/Squad leaderboards with **Nudge (`تنبيه التزام`)**, **Cheer (`تشجيع`)**, 1-tap **WhatsApp** invites/nudges/credential sharing, isolated per-user local state snapshots, cross-tab `BroadcastChannel` sync, and Vercel Serverless + **Supabase REST / Upstash KV** permanent cloud storage (`/api/coach` and `/api/squad`).
- **🎨 Shareable "Nawwa Victory Card" (1080×1080 PNG Poster):** Generates a custom bilingual dark-emerald trophy poster on HTML5 `<canvas>` with your Name, Tree Stage (`🌰 -> 🌴`), Streak, Points, and Quran Khatmah progress for native sharing or instant download.
- **🗓️ 30-Day "Seed Garden" Heatmap & Daily Gratitude Log:** Visualizes your 30-day habit intensity inside **Weekly AI Insights** and includes a daily **Gratitude & Mood Check-In (`سجل الشكر والامتنان`)** (`+10 pts`) that feeds directly into the AI Coach's memory.
- **🌙 Hijri Calendar Date, Sunnah Fasting (`صيام الاثنين والخميس / الأيام البيض`) & Sadaqah Tracker:** Displays the live Hijri date, Suhoor/Iftar timings, and 1-tap logging for Sunnah Fasting (`+25 pts`) and Daily Sadaqah (`+15 pts`).
- **☀️ Morning Briefing (`إشراقة الصباح`) & 🌙 Evening Review (`محاسبة المساء`):** 1-tap guided voice rituals that read aloud your Hijri date, next prayer time, and #1 easiest 2-minute Bronze seed in the morning, and review your completed habits at night.
